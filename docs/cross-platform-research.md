# Cross-Platform Autonomy Research Plan

**Status:** Proposed research direction

## Purpose

SCRAPYARD Labs is preparing to evaluate how a shared autonomy and operator-support approach can transfer between two very different robotic platforms:

- **W.R.A.I.T.H.** — Wheeled Robotic Autonomous Tactical Hauler, a custom platform built on a Quantum 600 powered-wheelchair base.
- **Unitree Go2** — a prospective legged platform, contingent on grant support.

## Software context

RAVEN Core v1.0.0 provides the existing foundation for voice interaction, operator visibility, computer-vision work, and learning and mission foundations for autonomous robotics.

The proposed research direction is to evaluate R.A.V.E.N. together with dimOS across both platforms. This is proposed work, not a claim of existing integration or tested performance.

## Research focus

The work would document:

- How autonomy and operator-support concepts transfer between wheeled and legged mobility
- How perception and operator-visibility workflows can be adapted for each platform
- Platform-specific integration requirements, constraints, and lessons learned
- Clear configuration notes and test outcomes as work is completed

## Documentation approach

SCRAPYARD Labs will distinguish current build status, planned integration, and verified results. Completed testing, performance claims, and cross-platform findings will only be published after they are documented.

## Current status

W.R.A.I.T.H. is the current custom platform associated with this research direction. A Unitree Go2 is prospective; no Go2 integration, testing, sponsorship, or partnership is claimed.
